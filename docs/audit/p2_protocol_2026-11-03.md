# P2 protocol: common evaluation of the 8 strategies (fixed before any P2 result was seen)

Written 2026-11-03, before `bensim/audit_p2.py` ran. Changing anything below after results exist makes it a new experiment.

- **Strategies and frozen parameters** (the code's own objects, unchanged):
  - fib618 = `weekly_check.LIVE`
  - imbalance = `books.IMB`
  - enigma = `books.ENIGMA` (H4)
  - h1_fvg = `fvg_strategy.FvgParams()`
  - bennett = `bennett_strategy.LIVE`
  - pullback = `PullbackParams()`
  - orderflow = `OrderFlowParams()`
  - gf = `GfParams()`
- **Symbols (all strategies):** EURUSD, GBPUSD, USDJPY, USDCHF, USDCAD, XAUUSD.
- **Period:** 2026-08-03 00:00 UTC to 2026-11-03 00:00 UTC, the last 3 months (user rule). Candles come from the DB after the insert-only backfill (data/audit/backfill_2026-11-03.json). The Oct 27 21:30–21:50 broker gap is kept as it is.
- **Execution, primary:** `exec_profile("observed_account")`. This is the demo account's observed spread, $0 commission and observed swap per lot-night, with a Friday 20:00 UTC close. Every cost is labelled observed / estimated / unknown in each report.
- **Execution, comparisons (reported, never used to pick anything):**
  1. `exec_profile("legacy")`: the pre-audit cost model, weekends held, no swap.
  2. observed_account without the Friday close: weekends held, as the live books do today.
- **Sizing:** R per trade, one position per strategy per symbol, strategies independent. The portfolio is the sum of R over all 8 (equal risk per trade). "Live set" = fib618 + imbalance + enigma + bennett.
- **Metrics:** bensim.validate: expectancy net and gross, PF, Sharpe/Sortino (daily), PSR, DSR (N = all registry trials), effective sample size, block bootstrap, drawdown and recovery, splits by pair, session, regime and month. Across strategies: PBO (CSCV, 8 blocks) and Holm-adjusted p-values.
- **Decision rule:** none. P2 is descriptive. No strategy is called an edge unless DSR > 0.95 **and** Holm p < 0.05 **and** the block-bootstrap P(total ≤ 0) < 0.05. Even then, it's only a candidate for an untouched-data test (P3-style), not for going live.
- **Not allowed:** changing parameters, symbols, period or costs after seeing results; dropping a strategy or symbol after the fact.
