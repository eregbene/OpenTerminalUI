## Chronology

## Rules

- Original level: clear support/resistance or supply/demand level.
- Origin: the first touch/origin must be an order block.
- Touches: at least three reactions. The mentor calls touches "reactions."
- Fakeout: price breaks through the level. Transcript and visuals support close-based fakeout; wicks alone are not enough for the implemented rule.
- Reclaim: price returns back through the level before entry.
- Entry: retest of reclaimed level.
- Stop: beyond fakeout/level. Mentor shows discretionary tight or wider stop.
- Targets: partials at intermediate levels/imbalances; full exit at opposing level taken.
- Avoid: huge fakeouts, because stop becomes too large.
- Premium/discount: not established in dedicated examples.
- HTF bias: not established in dedicated examples.
