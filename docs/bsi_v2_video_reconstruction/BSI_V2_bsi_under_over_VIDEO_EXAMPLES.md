# BSI V2 Under/Over Video Examples

Source basis: original mentor videos 19-20.

## UNDER_OVER_GOLDEN_01

Video 19, GBPUSD short.

- Level: resistance with at least three touches.
- Break: candle close above resistance.
- Reclaim: candle close back below resistance.
- Entry: at/near reclaimed level.
- Stop: above level/fakeout area.
- Management: partials on the way down.
- Full close: when opposing target level is taken out.
- Observed RR readouts include about 3.13 / 3.97 depending on measurement moment.

## UNDER_OVER_GOLDEN_02

Video 20, GBPUSD long.

- Level: support with at least three touches.
- Origin: first-touch candle boxed as order-block-quality anchor.
- Break: candle close below support; wicks do not count.
- Reclaim: candle close back above support.
- Entry: reclaimed level.
- Stop: below fakeout/level box, with tighter and wider variants shown.
- Management: partials at intermediate levels/FVGs.
- Full close: when final opposing level is taken out.

## UNDER_OVER_REJECT_01

Video 20, avoided second setup.

- Pattern resembles another Under/Over opportunity.
- Mentor rejects it because the fakeout is too large.
- Reason: required stop would be huge.
- Rule impact: fakeout size is qualitative; no numeric threshold is established.
