# BSI V2 ABC Video Examples

Source basis: original mentor videos 15-17 via audiovisual rulebooks and frame audits.

## ABC_GOLDEN_01

Video 15, summary/schematic.

- On-screen rule: wait for price to reclaim the A level and break the structure created in the C leg.
- Entry: identify an order block or FVG created by that break.
- Target: high/low of the B leg.
- Key distinction: this is not New York bare retest; OB/FVG is mandatory.

## ABC_GOLDEN_02

Video 16, short setup.

- A leg: bearish impulse.
- B leg: bullish pullback that does not invalidate A.
- C leg: bearish continuation that creates the reclaim and break.
- Entry: candle-anchored OB/FVG rectangle.
- Stop: beyond the selected array.
- Target: B-leg low/high depending on direction.
- Observed RR: 5.62.

## ABC_GOLDEN_03

Video 17, repeated live-chart example.

- Setup: ABC geometry on a regular pair/timeframe.
- Entry: visible rectangle at structure-break location.
- Target: B-leg high/low.
- Observed RR examples include 4.74 and 4.21 in the course notes.
- Mentor scope note: every pair.

## ABC_RECURSIVE_PATTERN

Across the ABC lesson, a completed ABC swing can seed the next larger ABC.

- Do not treat one completed ABC as permanently exhausted.
- Do not allow infinite chaining without new valid A/B/C structure.
- Each reentry still needs the full reclaim, C-leg structure break, and OB/FVG entry sequence.
