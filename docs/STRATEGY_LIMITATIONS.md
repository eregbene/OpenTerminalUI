# Strategy Limitations

Known Phase 6 limitations:

- Evaluations are process-local and not persisted.
- Exit lifecycle rules are modeled but not fully executed.
- Multi-timeframe contexts are modeled but not populated from separate datasets.
- Proposal overlays are shown in the inspector, not drawn as native chart price lines.
- No broker integration, order submission, fill simulation, AI strategy generation, or ML strategy selection.
- Full strategy research, optimization, and validation workflows are deferred to Phase 7.
