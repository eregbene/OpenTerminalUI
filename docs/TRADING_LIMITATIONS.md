# Trading Limitations

- No Interactive Brokers integration.
- No external broker connection.
- No live trading.
- No AI approval or autonomous deployment.
- File-backed persistence is used for the Phase 8 vertical slice.
- Margin, borrow, financing, settlement and institutional FX accounting are simplified.
- Daily-loss counters and correlation groups have policy foundations but limited historical state.
- Legacy paper trading remains active for compatibility and is not yet migrated to the canonical path.
