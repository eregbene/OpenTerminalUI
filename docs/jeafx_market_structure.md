## Minute-by-minute notes

**00:00–01:45 Three phases.** Uptrend (green box), consolidation (grey box), downtrend (red box). Buy only in uptrends,
sell only in downtrends, sit out consolidations ("no clear direction"). Stay with the trend until it signals the change.

**01:45–02:40 No trend lines.** A trend line break (circled) while price keeps rising = arbitrary line, no information.
Use raw highs and lows instead.

**02:40–05:30 High/low structure.** HH = high above the previous high; HL = each low above the previous low. HLs
"protect the integrity of the trend"; buy around the HLs. Price closing through the last HL → LL → "integrity broken" →
usually an LH follows → now sell around the next LH (05:00 drawing: hh/hl … LL, LH, red path continuing down).

**05:30–11:10 Real chart, EURCHF 4H.** He marks only the big swings (red arrows at the extreme high and low). A low that
does NOT go under the previous HL = consolidation, not a trend change (06:00–06:40 grey box). The push that closes under
that HL = start of the downtrend (LL), then LH, LL. A high above the last LH = HH = shift to up (08:00). 09:50–10:40:
lows inside a range that never made a new high are NOT higher lows — "anywhere between these two red lines is sideways";
the HL is the LOWEST point before the market broke the high. "Average out" to the simple individual highs and lows.

**11:25–22:50 Impulse vs correction.** Impulse = the pro-trend leg that makes the new HH (or LL) — it pushes price
over a high / under a low. Correction = the pull-back that stays INSIDE the impulse's range (does not break its high or
low). Impulses decide the direction; a correction is where to buy (uptrend) / sell (downtrend) — "buy as low as
possible in the corrective move". 17:40–22:50 EURCHF colour-coded: green = impulse, red = correction; a leg that
neither makes a new high nor a new low = consolidation (grey box, "a phase we would want to not trade"); the 22:40
frame shows uptrend (green) / downtrend (red) / uptrend boxes.

**23:00–26:00 Break of structure (BOS).** BOS = every time a high (low) is broken into a new high (low); the BOS line is
drawn from the previous high to the candle that breaks it. He marks BOS in both directions and refuses "CHoCH" (24:20
frame: CHoCH crossed out) — the first BOS against the trend is just a "reversal break".

**26:00–32:20 BOS on AUDJPY 1H (replay).** A BOS only counts when a candle CLOSES beyond the level ("where we start to
close above the previous high creates our first BOS"). After each BOS there are two levels: a close above the last high
→ continuation; a close below the protected low → downtrend. 29:20–30:40: a WICK under the low and a WICK over the high
→ "no man's land" (red box drawn between them) = consolidation; no trade until a close one way. 31:00: close under the
low → BOS down → downtrend.

**33:00–38:50 Mistake 1: internal structure.** Everything between the impulse's low and high is internal. A small LL
inside a pull-back is NOT a trend change; selling it gets you "killed" when the next impulse comes (36:00 drawing). Real
change = a new HH first, then a close under the HL that made that HH. GBPCAD 30M (36:20–38:50): a "BOS" over small
highs inside a fall is an illusion — the only valid low is the impulse's low and the only valid high is where the
impulse started; until that high closes, keep selling (price then made new lows).

**38:50–41:50 Mistake 2: liquidity sweep vs BOS.** Sweep = push above/below a swing that takes the orders and reverses
sharply. Tell them apart by CLOSURES: wick through = rejection = sweep (often reverses against you); body close
through = BOS. "If there is no candle closure above the level don't buy; no closure below don't sell." 41:20–41:40: high
swept twice with wicks (X marks), low swept with a wick (X) → no trend change.

**42:00–45:20 Supply & demand (diagrams).** In an uptrend the correction bottoms out in a short sideways BASE before
the impulse that breaks the high: that base = demand zone ("where significant buying came in"; institutions drive the
impulse). Drawn as a box from the base low (= the HL) to the top of the base, extended right; arrow "GOOD PLACE TO
BUY". Price often returns to the zone before the next push ("sometimes it just runs away and you miss it"). Trade:
buy at the zone, stop under the zone's low, target new highs (45:00: long box, grey stop under zone, blue target to the
high). Supply = mirror, only in downtrends (44:00, 45:20 drawings).

**45:40–54:00 NZDJPY 15M replay — the simple method.**
- Zone = "the last candle before the impulse that has imbalance" — the last candle before the push that broke
  structure; box over its range (46:40 close-up: box from that candle's wick to its other end).
- Imbalance = open price range: the wicks of the following candles never traded back into that candle → magnet. A zone
  whose gap got filled by a later wick is skipped (two such zones dismissed at 47:50–48:20).
- Use the LAST imbalanced zone of the leg (closest to price).
- Sell limit at the zone, stop above the swing high, target the leg's low (49:15: short box, grey stop above, blue to
  the low; "you could extend the target but for simplicity target the low"). Win: price came back to the zone and fell
  to the low (+3.94%).
- Next supply after the new push (50:00) → trade stopped: −1% (50:40 X). "50% win rate, still +2.94%".
- That loss closed over the last lower high → BOS up → he immediately re-maps the trend (LL, LH, HH = reversal) and
  takes the new DEMAND zone = last candle before the impulse that broke structure (52:00): buy limit at zone top, stop
  under the low, target the new high (or further). Filled after internal chop, +5.42% (53:45). Total +8.3% / 3 trades
  at 1% risk ("we can be wrong 50–60% but the winners are bigger").

**54:00–59:00 Standard confirmation entry (GBPUSD 10M → 5M).** For when you're nervous or have several zones: the
higher-TF downtrend made a big impulse; supply zone at its start (10M). Price corrects up in a slow channel and reaches
the zone. Drop to 5M: the 5M uptrend (HL, HH) must flip — a 5M candle CLOSES below the last 5M higher low (57:10) =
"high time frame and low time frame agreement". Sell limit at the new 5M supply (last 5M candle before the drop, with
imbalance), stop above the HTF zone / swing high (11 pips, 57:55), target the HTF low → ~5R (refined "10+R"). "Many
opportunities to take partial profits, go break even or stop trail along the way."

**59:00–68:40 Swing structure vs sub-structure (gold 4H → 30M).** Swing structure = the HTF trend (4H). Inside every 4H
correction there is a lower-TF counter-trend (30M) = sub-structure. 4H gold: BOS up over the last LH → buy limit at the
lowest imbalanced 4H demand (the base before the BOS impulse, red box). Meanwhile on 30M: wait for a CLOSE under the
30M higher low (a wick is not enough, 64:40), then sell — (1) at the close under the low, stop above the next high, or
(2) at the newly formed 30M supply zone after the close. Target = the 4H demand zone only ("never push a sub-structure
trade past an area of demand"; "sell to buy"). When the 30M sell reaches the 4H zone, the buy limit there fills and
rides the 4H impulse to new highs (67:20–68:20, X on the first premature entry).

## His rules, condensed
1. Trend = only the big swings. HL = lowest low before the close above the previous HH (mirror for LH). Everything
   between the current protected low and the last high is internal / correction.
2. BOS = candle CLOSE beyond the protected level (or the last extreme). Wick only = sweep/consolidation → no trade.
3. Phase: UP, DOWN, or CONSOLIDATION (wick breaks both ways, or no new extreme) → stand aside in consolidation.
4. Zone = last candle before the impulse that made the BOS (the base at the protected swing), box over that candle's
   range; valid only while its imbalance is open (no later wick back into it); use the newest valid zone; one use.
5. Entry = limit at the zone's near edge; stop beyond the protected swing; target = the impulse's extreme (≥ ~3R in his
   examples), optionally further.
6. After a loss that closes through the protected level: flip immediately and trade the new zone in the new direction.
7. Confirmation option: HTF zone tapped → LTF close through the LTF HL/LH → limit at the new LTF zone, stop above the
   HTF zone, target the HTF extreme.
8. Sub-structure option: inside an HTF correction, LTF BOS against the HTF trend → counter-trend trade only down to the
   HTF zone, then the HTF limit takes over.
9. Risk 1% per trade; win rate ~50% is fine because R is 3–5.

## New strategy: STRUCTURE FLOW (sflow) — one state machine, three entries

Not Bennett (no OTE/fib, no box engine, no premium/discount, no FVG list) and not Phantom (zones are not every order
block; only the base of the impulse that made the current BOS; stop at the protected swing; flip rule; sell-to-buy).

State per symbol and per timeframe pair (HTF, LTF) — default (H1, M5) and (H4, M15) to cover his examples:

1. **Structure (HTF, closed bars only).** Track `ext_high`, `ext_low`, `protected` and `phase`. Up: a close above
   `ext_high` = BOS up → `protected` = lowest low since the previous BOS (the HL), `ext_high` updates as price runs.
   A close below `protected` = BOS down → phase DOWN (mirror). A wick beyond either level without a close → phase stays,
   flag `swept`. Phase CONSOL when the last 2 levels were only swept (wick both sides) and no close since.
2. **Zone.** At each BOS: the impulse = bars from the protected swing to the BOS close; zone candle = the last candle
   before the impulse's first displacement bar (the base candle at the protected swing); zone = that candle's
   high..low. Valid while no later wick has traded back into the zone's gap (the range between the zone candle and the
   next candles' extreme). Keep only the newest valid zone in the trend direction.
3. **Entry A — zone limit.** Limit at the zone's near edge; stop = protected swing ± 0.1 ATR; target = `ext_high`
   (`ext_low`) at the time of the order. Cancel if price reaches the target first, if the zone's imbalance is consumed
   without a fill (wick through to the far side), or if the protected level closes (BOS against).
4. **Entry B — confirmation.** Same zone, but no resting limit: when an HTF bar trades into the zone, run the same
   structure engine on the LTF; when the LTF closes through its own protected level in the HTF direction, place the
   limit at the new LTF zone; stop above the HTF zone's far edge (his 11-pip stop), target = the HTF extreme.
   Expiry: 24 HTF bars after the tap.
5. **Flip.** A close through `protected` → phase flips; the new impulse's zone is armed at once (his −1% → +5.42%).
6. **Entry C — sell-to-buy (optional, off by default).** Phase UP on HTF, price correcting: LTF close under its HL →
   sell at the LTF zone, stop above the LTF swing high, target = the HTF demand zone's near edge; skip if < 1.5R room.
7. **Rules kept from his talk.** No trade in CONSOL; never trade an internal break; one trade per zone; risk 1%
   (engine uses our live risk); no partials/BE in v1 (his "optional" — tested separately: BE hurt fib618).
